# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-15

First playable version.

- Loads any Doom 1 IWAD (shareware DOOM1.WAD, registered DOOM.WAD or The Ultimate Doom)
  and rebuilds every ExMy map as a block world seen from a Minecraft Dungeons style camera.
- Real map geometry, wall and floor textures, animated nukage and water, sector lighting
  with flickering, strobing and glowing lights.
- Monsters, items, keys and decorations turned into voxel models from the WAD's own sprites,
  using Doom's 8-way rotation frames.
- All ten Doom 1 monsters with Doom's stats, attacks, pain chances, infighting, sound
  propagation and Nightmare respawning.
- All eight weapons with Doom's damage, spread, ammo and timings, plus aim assist for the
  top-down view.
- Doors (including keyed and timed), lifts, moving floors, crushers, stairs, donuts,
  teleporters, switches, secret areas, damaging floors, normal and secret exits.
- Doom's sound effects from the WAD with distance falloff and stereo panning; MUS music
  played through a Web Audio synthesizer.
- Status bar face, automap and minimap, intermission tally with par times, episode endings.
